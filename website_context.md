This document contains the complete context, business information,
website research, strategy, sitemap, UX direction, content direction,
and implementation blueprint for redesigning the Selective Vacations
website.

Use this document as the master context for an AI website builder such
as Antigravity, ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, or a web-design/development
agent.

IMPORTANT: - Treat the two supplied client documents as the primary
source for current business positioning and owner requirements. - Treat
the existing Selective Vacations website as the source for
historical/current tour inventory and legacy content. - Do NOT invent
missing business facts. - Any contact details, certifications, customer

you want to experience it, and we'll design the journey."

If you are using an AI website builder, follow these principles:

1.  Build a polished, production-quality travel website, not a generic
    AI template.
2.  Use the supplied business context as the source of truth.
3.  Prioritize visual storytelling.
4.  Use premium India photography.
5.  Make the homepage conversion-focused.
6.  Keep navigation simple.
7.  Make destinations visually prominent.
8.  Make personalization the main differentiator.
9.  Make Shailendra a visible human trust element.
10. Make WhatsApp/call/contact actions immediately accessible.
11. Build responsive layouts.
12. Use subtle, premium animation.
13. Use generous spacing.
14. Avoid clutter.
15. Avoid generic travel-agency visual patterns.
16. Use consistent typography and spacing.
17. Build reusable components for destination cards, journey cards,
    trust blocks, testimonials and CTA sections.
18. Ensure every journey can have a "Customize" action.
19. Do not use placeholder facts as if they are real.
20. Mark missing client information clearly until it is provided.

# 43. FINAL QUALITY BAR

The finished site should make a visitor think:

> "These people know India."

Then:

> "They can build the trip around me."

Then:

> "I can actually talk to a real person."

Then:

> "I feel comfortable contacting them."

The website should NOT primarily make visitors think:

> "Here is another website with hundreds of tour packages."

The primary experience should be:

DISCOVER → TRUST → IMAGINE → CUSTOMIZE → CONTACT

Build a complete modern website redesign for Selective Vacations, a
specialist India domestic travel company.

The current website is: http://selectivevacations.com/

The business specializes in customized India journeys across North and
South India, including famous destinations and specialist travel
categories.

> INDIA, CURATED AROUND YOU.

deep India knowledge, carefully selected hotels/transportation and 24/7

image carousel - Golden Triangle - Taj Mahal - South India - Kerala -

support - Featured journeys - Testimonials - Strong contact/lead CTA

Primary conversion: \> Plan My Trip

Secondary: \> WhatsApp Us

Tertiary: \> Call Us

Travel Styles - Travel Style pages - Featured Journeys - Journey detail
pages - Why Selective - About - Shailendra - Services - Journal -
Contact - Plan My Trip
